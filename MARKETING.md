# OneWeave Marketing Content

**App Name:** OneWeave  
**Tagline:** Life as One Interconnected Journey.

## Short App Store Description
OneWeave weaves your life into one living story. Track energy, habits, resources, relationships, and legacy in four interconnected Life Threads. See real ripples across domains on the Journey Compass, with live energy bars, trends, and "Active Ripples." Privacy-first and on-device. Calm, minimalist design that respects your attention.

## Full Description (for website / App Store)
Life isn't a collection of separate apps and to-do lists. It's one woven journey.

OneWeave models your world as four fluid Life Threads — Self (energy & habits), Stewardship (resources & leaks), Care & Kin (tasks & relationships), and Meaning & Legacy (stories & purpose). Every action creates ripples that flow across threads, updating your central Journey Compass with dynamic energy visualization, insight, and real-world bridges.

See the immediate impact of a busy-season goal on your care load. Watch a subscription leak free resources for legacy work. Capture anything quickly and watch it route intelligently while updating the whole picture.

Built for clarity, not overwhelm:
- Event-driven interconnections that actually surface cross-domain effects
- Energy-aware Compass with per-thread bars and recent trend visualization
- Active Ripples summary showing live cross-thread consequences
- Calm, minimalist SwiftUI interface
- Fully local SwiftData — nothing leaves your device by default
- Journey simulations that demonstrate real-life scenarios (busy seasons, leaks, high care loads, resource redirects)

Stop managing life in silos. Weave it.

## Key Benefits
- See the hidden connections between your goals, money, family, and purpose
- Make better decisions with live energy and ripple awareness
- Reduce overwhelm through a single calm view instead of 10 fragmented apps
- Privacy-first: all data stays local; no training on your life
- Build real momentum by understanding how actions in one area affect everything else

## Target Audience
Thoughtful adults who feel life is interconnected but lack a single system to see and act on the ripples. Professionals managing energy and goals, parents balancing care loads, anyone seeking legacy or meaning while handling daily life. Ideal for users who value calm design, privacy, and systems thinking.

- **History & Threads Overview** — Full timeline of ripples + quick summaries of all threads
- **Privacy & Control** — Local-only SwiftData, clear data, on-device insights toggle
- **Demo Journeys** — Pre-built simulations of realistic life scenarios
