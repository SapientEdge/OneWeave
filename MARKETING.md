## Full App Store Description

**OneWeave — Your life. One woven story.**

OneWeave is the first life operating system built on a real knowledge graph of your life. It models your world as four interconnected Life Threads — Self, Stewardship, Care & Kin, Meaning & Legacy — and shows you the actual ripples between them.

**What makes OneWeave different:**

🧭 **The Journey Compass** — A living visual of all four threads, with energy bars, recent trends, and "Active Ripples" showing what's affecting what, right now.

🕸️ **The Living Graph Loom** — Your life rendered as a breathing constellation. Tap any entity to see its relationships. Watch decisions ripple through your graph in real time.

🪦 **Sacred Echo Vault** — Encrypted time-capsule messages. Leave a letter for your daughter to open on her wedding day. Set a video message to release if you receive a medical diagnosis. AES-256-GCM. Fail-closed crypto. No plaintext on disk.

👁️ **Invisible Mentor** — Your past self, quoted back to you when you face decisions. Pure local. No LLM. No Core ML. No network. Just your own reflections, surfaced at the moment you need them.

🛑 **Weave Pause** — When your cognitive load rises above 0.85 AND your body shows depletion, OneWeave asks — gently — if you should pause the next commitment. You can dismiss it. It's a friend, not a dictator.

🧠 **Cognitive Load Score** — Six components (calendar, quests, threads, sleep, reflection-days, amplifier load) combined into a single 0-1 metric with rising/steady/falling trend detection.

👨‍👩‍👧 **Family Pod** — Share with up to 6 people. Quiet hours. Cooldowns. Per-member privacy defaults. Anti-social by design: notification only when it matters.

📋 **Decision Log + Mentor Bridge** — When you make a decision, OneWeave records your reasoning and predicted outcome. Later, when the outcome is known, it bridges back to your Invisible Mentor: "Last time you decided X, you said Y. Does that still hold?"

✍️ **Quick Capture Inbox** — A single field. Type anything. It's automatically classified as a task, event, journal, decision, or note. Pure heuristic. No LLM. No latency.

🌅 **Morning Briefing / Evening Review** — Today at a glance. Tonight's reflection. Generated locally from your own data.

🔒 **Privacy Data Leash** — One matrix, nine integration toggles. Calendar, Contacts, HealthKit, Reminders, Mail, Notes, Body Thread, P2P, Insights. Each gated individually. Each read live from your settings BEFORE any data access.

**No accounts. No cloud. No training on your life.** The free tier is generous — full local app, unlimited journal entries, full graph. The optional paid tier ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr) unlocks cross-device sync via your own iCloud Drive (not our servers), extended export formats, and priority support.
