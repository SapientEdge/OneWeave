# OneWeave Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-27

OneWeave is built privacy-first (PbD principles).

**No data leaves your device.** All storage is local SwiftData. No network, no analytics, no telemetry, no model training on your data.

**What we store (all on-device):**
- Life threads, events, quests (with reflections), essence, mastery tiers, harmony, streaks (with grace), ledger, seasonal state.

**Your controls:**
- Full export (JSON v2 including gamif).
- Clear all data at any time.
- Reflection gates require conscious IRL action.

**Widgets/Live Activities:**
- Use on-device snapshots only.

**No third parties.** No accounts. No cloud.

For questions: review source or Settings export.

This app exists to help you weave your life — on your terms, on your device.
